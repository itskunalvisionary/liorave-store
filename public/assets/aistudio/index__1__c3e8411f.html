How We Began

A house built on craft, not shortcuts

Lioravé began in a small atelier with a simple frustration — that fine-looking jewellery so often meant compromising on comfort, ethics, or price. We set out to make pieces that felt as considered as an heirloom, without the wait or the weight.

Today, every design passes through the same hands that sketched our very first ring: checked, finished, and packed with the same care.

— The Lioravé Atelier
